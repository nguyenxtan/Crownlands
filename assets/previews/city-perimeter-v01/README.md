# City perimeter · concept 01

User request: “tạo dựng hình ảnh trước đi”. Two separate built-in image_gen outputs, preview only:

- Wall: `assets/source/city-wall-v01/proof-v01.png`, 1448×1086, six watchtowers, south entrance opening. Exact sibling prompt and generation/hash record retained.
- Gate: `assets/source/city-gate-v01/proof-v01.png`, 1774×887, two flanking towers, open timber doors and level passage. Exact sibling prompt and generation/hash record retained.

Open [index.html](index.html) for the review board, [composite.png](composite.png) for the trial composition, or [review-sheet.png](review-sheet.png) for the whole board. Source references are the approved hall, summer ground v02 and the generated wall for gate consistency. The six wall towers plus two gate towers make eight in the assembled concept.

Both images are true RGBA with transparent corners. Browser pixel checks confirm the wall's empty center and the gate's open passage are transparent. Wall: 85.36% fully transparent pixels, 12.68% with alpha≥240; gate: 51.44% fully transparent pixels, 45.93% with alpha≥240. Seven image references on the review page decode successfully. The composition is an HTML/browser screenshot; source images have not been cropped, repainted or flattened into a new production asset.

These drafts are not integrated into the game or promoted to approved production exports. Exact full-canvas 2048×1536 perimeter alignment, the camera/ellipse fit, building overlap and foreground/background rendering still need production work. The preview's hall placement/scale illustrates the concept and is not a claim that all 14 building/16 resource anchors are ready with this wall. The app retains its temporary wall/gate while the separate navigation bugfix is delivered.
